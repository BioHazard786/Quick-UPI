Stop fumbling. Start receiving.

Quick UPI is the fastest way to request payments on Android. Forget opening heavy banking apps, navigating endless menus, and waiting for server connections just to show your QR code.

Simply swipe down, tap the Quick Settings Tile, enter an amount, and boom—you're ready to get paid.

🚀 USE CASES:

• The "Split Bill" Speedster: Flash a custom QR for exactly your share before friends can even unlock their phones.
• Delivery Agents: Generate exact amount QRs instantly. No more disputes over change.
• Taxi & Auto Drivers: Create dynamic QRs for every ride.
• Small Shop Owners: Type amount, show QR, next customer.
• Privacy Pro: Receive payments without shouting your phone number.

✨ FEATURES:

• Quick Settings Tile: Instant access from your notification shade.
• Zero Lag: App opens instantly.
• Custom Amounts & Notes: Add context like "Dinner" or "Cab".
• Recent Amounts: Smart chips remember your frequent values.
• Offline Generation: No internet required to create the QR code.
• Material 3 Design: Sleek, modern, and beautiful.
• Privacy First: Your data stays on your device.

The app is completely open source and respects your privacy. No ads, no tracking.
